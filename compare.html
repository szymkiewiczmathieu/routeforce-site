Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest Salesforce-native route planning app?

Among Salesforce-native options with public pricing, Tourvia costs €30 excluding tax per licensed user per month, billed annually. Salesforce publishes Maps at $75 per user per month and Maps Advanced at $150 per user per month, billed annually (salesforce.com, checked 26 July 2026). Geopointe requires a quote.

Which route planning apps are Salesforce-native?

Salesforce Maps, Geopointe, and Tourvia are delivered as Salesforce managed packages. Badger Maps, SPOTIO, and Route4Me are standalone applications that connect to Salesforce through a sync or connector.

What replaces the Salesforce Maps mobile app after August 31, 2026?

Salesforce retires the standalone Salesforce Maps mobile app on August 31, 2026. Tourvia runs the route-to-visit workflow on Salesforce records with mobile check-in and visit reports written as Salesforce Events, at €30 excluding tax per licensed user per month, billed annually.
