Product name update

RouteForce is now Tourvia

Tourvia is the new product name for the Salesforce-native route planning app previously called RouteForce.

  • Published28 May 2026
  • Updated16 July 2026
  • SectionProduct name update
  • AuthorSKZ Consulting, the team behind Tourvia

RouteForce now uses the product name Tourvia.

The company behind the product is SKZ Consulting. RouteForce is the former product and company name. Tourvia keeps the software distinct while the app continues to do the same job inside Salesforce: map CRM records, optimize routes, create visits, and track field execution.

If you searched for RouteForce, you are in the right place. The product is now called Tourvia.

What changes

The name changes. The product direction does not.

  • The 30-day trial request stays the same.
  • The Salesforce managed package keeps the same core workflow.
  • Route planning, Campaign Member mapping, GPS check-in, visit reports, dashboards, and per-user pricing remain the focus.
  • Gettourvia.com is now the primary public site. Routeforce.app remains available during the migration.

Why the new name matters

Tourvia gives the product its own name while staying clear about what it does: route planning and field execution for Salesforce teams.

During the transition, older RouteForce references may still appear in screenshots, technical object names, package internals, or historical articles. Public product naming will move toward Tourvia.

Where to go next

New buyers should evaluate Tourvia. Existing RouteForce links and articles will continue to point to the right product during the transition.
