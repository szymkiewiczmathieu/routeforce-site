Salesforce field-tool pricing: what per-user rates cost in 2026

Salesforce field tools often use the same basic model: a monthly price for each licensed user, billed annually. The seat price and included workflow determine the final budget.

1. How per-user rates scale

Most Salesforce field tools use a per-user, per-month licensing model. The model is easy to calculate, but each additional seat raises annual spend. When the seat price is high, teams may limit rollout even when more users need the workflow.

Take Salesforce Maps as an example. According to Salesforce’s US pricing page, checked 5 September 2026:

  • Salesforce Maps: $75/user/month
  • Salesforce Maps Advanced (includes route optimization): the pricing card showed $125/user/month on 5 September 2026, while an FAQ on the same page said $150. Verify current edition pricing directly with Salesforce.

For a single rep, $75/month is manageable. But field teams are rarely one person. Here is what happens as your team grows:

  • 10 users on Salesforce Maps: $750/month = $9,000/year just for mapping
  • 10 users on Maps Advanced: $1,250/month = $15,000/year using the pricing-card figure
  • 20 users on Maps Advanced: $2,500/month = $30,000/year using the pricing-card figure

The cost grows linearly with every licensed rep. At the $125 pricing-card figure, three additional Maps Advanced licences add $4,500 to a full-year calculation. Confirm proration and contract amendment terms in the vendor quote.

5. Compare the same pricing model at the right scope

Salesforce Maps may fit when:

  • Your team is very small (1 to 3 reps) and will stay that way
  • You specifically need Salesforce Maps territory analytics or territory planning features
  • Your organization already has an enterprise agreement that includes Maps at a discount

Tourvia may fit when:

  • You have 5 or more field reps and the number is growing
  • Your team size fluctuates seasonally (temporary reps, contractors, new hires)
  • You want a lower published seat price and can calculate the annual total from the expected licence count
  • Your primary need is operational: route planning, check-ins, visit tracking, reporting
  • You want every rep to have access without justifying each seat to finance

At the published prices in this article, Tourvia has a lower annual seat cost. Compare currencies, taxes, negotiated discounts, implementation, and feature scope before treating the totals as equivalent quotes.
