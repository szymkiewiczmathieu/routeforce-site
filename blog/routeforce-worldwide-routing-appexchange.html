Tourvia field execution with international route planning

Tourvia sharpens the product around one clear promise: start from the 30-day trial request in Salesforce, then subscribe to keep route optimization and richer field execution when your team is ready.

  • Updated21 July 2026

Plan and report visits from Salesforce

Tourvia connects route planning with Salesforce Events, mobile visits, and visit reports. The relevant Salesforce records remain the working context before and after the route calculation.

Worldwide route planning

Tourvia supports route planning for Salesforce teams operating across countries and regions.

International routing is available where the required road data exists. Coverage and route quality can vary by territory, so test the countries and regions required by the field team during the trial.

What stays connected to the route

  • Salesforce-native workflow instead of a disconnected external tool
  • Private AppExchange access coordinated with the customer's Salesforce admin
  • One published seat price for the complete feature set
  • Field execution with visit reports, GPS-assisted proximity checks, and mobile use inside Salesforce

EU data hosting and routing infrastructure

Tourvia's routing infrastructure is hosted in France. The minimum coordinates and timing constraints required for route calculation are processed there under the terms described in the DPA. Compare that documented data flow with each vendor's own contractual information.

What to test during the trial

A Salesforce admin can install Tourvia, assign the supplied Permission Set Groups, and validate the complete route-to-visit workflow without adding another field platform.

Tourvia is distributed through private AppExchange access coordinated with the customer’s Salesforce admin. A requested 30-day trial includes route optimization, GPS check-in, and configurable visit reports. The subscription is EUR 30 excluding tax per licensed user per month, billed annually.

Use the trial with real Salesforce records and a small field group before assigning licences to the wider team.

What Tourvia supports in the field

Tourvia supports the full field workflow inside Salesforce:

  • route planning inside Salesforce
  • visit planning inside Salesforce
  • field execution inside Salesforce
  • GPS-assisted proximity check-in
  • visit reports with configurable fields
  • nearby accounts and proximity workflows
  • mobile use inside Salesforce mobile
  • configurable workflows powered by Screen Flows and Lightning Web Components
