Salesforce does not endorse or recommend Tourvia. Passing Security Review means the package met the security requirements Salesforce publishes for AppExchange distribution. The two are not the same.

What it means

Salesforce reviews every managed package before it can be distributed on AppExchange. The review covers authentication, data access, sharing rules, CRUD and FLS enforcement, secret handling, external callouts, and how the package behaves inside customer orgs.

TourviaApp 7.25.0 passed that review on May 22, 2026. The 7.34.0.1 build is built from the same base.

What it does not mean

Passing Security Review does not mean Salesforce endorses or recommends Tourvia. It means the package met Salesforce's published security requirements for AppExchange distribution.

For IT, security, and procurement teams, the useful questions are:

  • Was the package reviewed before it installs into a customer org?
  • Does it enforce sharing, CRUD, and FLS?
  • Does it handle secrets and external callouts the way AppExchange expects?
  • Is it distributed through the AppExchange pipeline, not as a metadata dump?

TourviaApp 7.25.0 answers yes to all four. Buyers still need to run their own due diligence on the current version, DPA, architecture, permissions, and external processing.

What Tourvia includes today

Tourvia brings route planning, Salesforce events, mobile check-in, visit reports, and dashboards into one managed package.

Route optimization inside Salesforce

The engine runs on Account, Contact, Lead, Opportunity, and custom geolocation objects. Reps plan and re-optimize routes without leaving Salesforce.

Campaign Member mapping

Lead and Contact campaign members appear on the map from a Campaign record or the global Tourvia workspace. Marker color is driven by Custom Metadata.

Visit planning

Visits become standard Salesforce events or Tourvia visit records, linked back to the originating Account, Contact, Lead, Opportunity, or Campaign Member.

Mobile field execution and GPS check-in

Reps run their day from the Salesforce mobile app. Tourvia records arrival and departure timestamps against the visit.

Visit reports and dashboards

Visit data is standard Salesforce data. The package includes prebuilt reports and dashboards that admins can clone and customize.
