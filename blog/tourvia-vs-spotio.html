Tourvia vs SPOTIO: which field sales route tool fits Salesforce better?

TourviaversusSPOTIO

SPOTIO is a popular field sales platform with route planning, activity tracking, and performance dashboards. Tourvia is a Salesforce managed package focused on route-to-visit execution inside Salesforce.

Start with SPOTIO's standalone field workflow

SPOTIO combines route planning, activity tracking, dashboards, and field-sales functions in its own application. When Salesforce is part of the architecture, verify the connector, supported objects, sync timing, conflict handling, API use, and reporting ownership.

SPOTIO supplies personalized pricing. Ask the vendor to identify licence, platform, integration, implementation, support, and renewal terms in the quote.

What to look for in a SPOTIO alternative

If you want to keep field execution inside Salesforce, prioritize:

  • Salesforce managed-package workflow and documented external processing
  • Route optimization from Salesforce records
  • Mobile app that creates Events and Tasks in Salesforce
  • Published per-user annual pricing
  • Implementation and training effort measured in the target Salesforce org

When to choose Tourvia over SPOTIO

Include Tourvia when reps should plan routes and record visits from Salesforce. Include SPOTIO when the team wants a separate field-sales application with its own activity and management workflow. Test both against the same Salesforce integration and mobile requirements.

Pricing comparison

Tourvia is €30 excluding tax per licensed user per month, billed annually. SPOTIO supplies a personalized quote. Compare the same seat count, contract period, implementation, connector, support, and renewal scope.

Frequently asked questions

Is SPOTIO Salesforce-native?

No. SPOTIO is a standalone field sales application that connects to Salesforce through a connector. Tourvia is a Salesforce managed package, so Salesforce stays the system of record for routes, visits, and reports.

How much does SPOTIO cost?

SPOTIO supplies personalized pricing; ask the vendor to itemize licence, platform, integration, implementation, support, and renewal terms. Tourvia publishes €30 excluding tax per licensed user per month, billed annually.

When does SPOTIO fit better than Tourvia?

SPOTIO fits teams that want a separate field sales application with its own activity tracking, dashboards, and management workflow. Tourvia fits teams that want reps to plan routes and record visits without leaving Salesforce.

Who owns visit data in each tool?

With SPOTIO, visit data lives in SPOTIO or is shared through the connector; verify supported objects, sync timing, and conflict handling. With Tourvia, visits are Salesforce Events created in your org, and route inputs are processed in France as described in the DPA.
