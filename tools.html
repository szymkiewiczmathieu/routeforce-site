Privacy boundary

The planning inputs stay in your browser.

The CSV checker processes its selected file in the browser tab. The capacity calculator processes the numbers you enter and creates its CSV locally. Neither utility uploads the source file, inputs or results to Tourvia.

Page analytics may record a visit to the page, but not the values you enter, calculate or download.

What these utilities do not do

  • No Salesforce access: neither tool reads or modifies your CRM.
  • No operating guarantee: geography, calendars, customer availability and route constraints still need a real workflow check.
  • No hidden score: outputs are aggregate signals or arithmetic from the inputs you provide.
