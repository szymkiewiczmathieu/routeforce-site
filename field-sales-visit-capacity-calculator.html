Read this result correctly

Capacity math is a planning prompt, not an operating forecast.

  • Includes: user-entered coverage demand, workdays, admin time, a contingency buffer, visit time and travel time.
  • Does not model: real geography, traffic, route sequence, appointment windows, no-shows, skill mix or actual territory assignments.
  • Does not conclude: which reps to hire, how many visits will occur, or what value a route tool will create.

Calculation method

Every number can be checked.

  1. Required visits = accounts in each tier × visits per period, summed across tiers.
  2. Minutes per visit = average visit duration + average travel time.
  3. Available team minutes = reps × field days × (field hours − admin hours) × 60 × (1 − buffer).
  4. Modeled visit slots = floor(available team minutes ÷ minutes per visit).
  5. Rep-equivalent demand = required visit minutes ÷ modeled minutes available per rep. It is a scenario ratio, not a staffing recommendation.

Interpret the gap

A positive balance does not prove a route will work.

The calculator only makes the aggregate constraint visible. A coverage policy may still fail in practice if visits cluster in the wrong places, appointment windows conflict, travel estimates are too low, or priorities change during the period.

Questions

Before you use a capacity scenario

Are the illustrative inputs recommended values?

No. They exist only to show the calculation. Replace every value with your own policy and operating assumptions before using the result.

Does the result tell me how many reps to hire?

No. The rep-equivalent number is a time ratio under the assumptions you entered. It does not account for skills, territories, hiring, absences, contracts, route feasibility or management decisions.

Does this calculate a route?

No. It makes an aggregate coverage constraint visible. Route sequence, appointment windows, real travel time and field-day feasibility need a map and operational validation.

Is Tourvia required to use this calculator?

No. This is a vendor-neutral planning utility. A Tourvia trial is a separate evaluation of route planning and Salesforce workflow in the target org.
